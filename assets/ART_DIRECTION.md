# Arte original — arquivos e prompts

Modo: ferramenta integrada image_gen (sem CLI/API no jogo). Os masters estão em assets/source/; os scripts de preparação geram a resolução de produção com paleta limitada e alpha binário. O jogo funciona totalmente offline.

## Integração e iluminação por horário

A grade final é 256×144, com ampliação inteira em pixels físicos. O personagem usa cabeça com espaço para chapéus, pernas dobradas, banco e sombra de contato dentro do casco. As animações de mascotes usam quatro poses em pixels com pés fixos; bandeiras usam dobras discretas e roupas preservam a luminância do sprite.

O relógio local do dispositivo determina a atmosfera: amanhecer 5h–8h, meio-dia 11h–14h, entardecer 17h–20h e noite. `pesca_luz.py` prepara 96 camadas fixas por material e interpola cores sem redesenhar grupos de pixels. Dia: céu azul suave e verde iluminado; amanhecer: lavanda e pêssego; meio-dia: água turquesa e destaques claros; entardecer: âmbar; noite: índigo e azul, com pontos quentes de cabana e lanterna. As mesmas silhuetas e alphas são preservados em todos os horários.

Pássaros e borboletas são eventos diurnos; vaga-lumes, coruja sonolenta e meteoros discretos aparecem no escuro. Sombras de peixes, ondulações, vegetação e reflexos do casco usam uma fase de animação independente do relógio civil. Os efeitos de acessórios foram suavizados para manter o clima acolhedor.

## Cenário

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished original background plate for a playable cozy fishing game, not concept art and not a screenshot of another game.
Create an exceptionally polished, hand-pixeled 16-bit RPG lakeside scene with the environmental craftsmanship, organic silhouettes, hue-shifted shadows and carefully clustered pixels of the finest SNES-era RPG backgrounds, especially Chrono Trigger. Entirely original geography, trees and building.

Output a widescreen 16:9 image. The design is on a strict 256 by 144 logical pixel grid, shown as a clean integer enlargement (prefer 1536 by 864, six output pixels per logical pixel). Crisp nearest-neighbor squares of consistent size throughout. No smooth gradients, no antialiasing, no painted blur, no pseudo-pixel noise. About 48-64 carefully selected colors. Rich deliberate clusters and selective contours rather than black outlines. Big areas should have material-specific detail and carefully spaced accents.

Composition in logical 256x144 coordinates: tranquil lake horizon near y=67. An extensive distant lavender and desaturated turquoise mountain range and hazy fir forests in the upper middle, soft apricot evening sky in the upper quarter. A broad, beautifully layered old forest canopy frames the upper left edge, with a gnarled tree trunk, visible curved branches, and several overlapping leaf clusters. Left shoreline curves from x=0,y=68 to x=74,y=84. On that shoreline a tiny original weathered fisherman's cabin with a mossy blue slate roof and one warmly lit amber window around x=49,y=56, and a narrow wooden dock ending at x=79,y=88. Right shore curves from x=256,y=60 to x=222,y=89, with lush trees, fern fronds, rounded moss-covered boulders, roots and grass.

Most important: KEEP THE CENTRAL OPEN WATER REGION x=80..214, y=84..124 free of all boats, people, fishing rods, fish, lanterns, structures and large plants. The runtime will place an animated boat and fisherman there. No characters or boats anywhere in the asset. Water must be deep teal in foreground, sage turquoise in distance, with broken horizontal reflections of shores, sparse ripples, subtle submerged stones near banks, physically coherent sunset reflections near x=205. Plenty of calm negative space for animated actors. A few small lily pads near bottom corners only. Foreground natural banks frame bottom left and bottom right with beautiful layered reeds, flowers, moss, ferns and larger shaded stones, leaving the lower middle water unobstructed.

Lighting: warm afternoon rim light from upper right on cliff facets, canopy edges, wood and roof, contrasted with cool blue-green shadow ramps. Cozy, memorable, lush, visually sumptuous yet clearly readable at the actual 256x144 size. Avoid geometric triangle mountains, rectangular cloud blocks, mechanical round tree blobs, horizontal uniform stripes, repetitively tiled dashes, grain, text, interface, labels, borders, logos and watermark.

Left arm resting near knee, readable separated anatomy. No hat or headwear: cosmetic hats are layered by the game. No extra costume items.

Head top around y=1. Comfortable seated profile silhouette with legs below and extending right. Transparent around the entire sprite, no ground shadow. Crisp uniform pixel grid, ideally show each logical pixel as an integer square enlargement. Deliberate pixel clusters, 24-32 colors, cool blue-violet shadows, warm amber rim light from upper right. Rich confident pixel shading, not flat rectangles, not minimal icon, not smooth digital painting, not vector art, not realistic anatomy.
